Where it came from

On 1 November 2026 a reskin of bespoke.gig began with a block of CSS called "Hypermetapunk Kit v0", parents Storeybrook × Depthfield, "written to be lifted out whole". Version 0.1 lifts it: the kit's names are kept, its contrast is fixed (no more white on orange), its type is open-licence, and it has a brand book, tokens, live components and a page check like its parents.

Next on the family plan: bespoke.gig rebuilt on the kit, a pen for AND SO's neon skin, and night pets that cross over from Depthfield.
