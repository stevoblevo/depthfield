Origami

Walk round: drag sideways. Move the sun: drag up or down.

Day is Storeybrook, dusk is Hypermetapunk, night is Depthfield: one world, and every look a material over it.

Play: tap the star and the seal heads it. Keep it up. The pets on the beach are the kits' own, dancing to the beat.
