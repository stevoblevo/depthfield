Five axes

x
Swipe sideways, or ← →. Turns the field.
depth
Swipe up, scroll, or ↓ ↑. The camera descends.
time
Press and hold, or Space. The clock stops. The slider also runs it backwards.
look
The slider, or L. Changes the weather: mint, peach fall, red rain, night garden, thread. Left on auto it follows the descent. Your pointer is where the focus goes and where the eye points.
memory
Tap, or M. Leaves a mark. Marks stay in this browser and nowhere else; the slider shows more or fewer of them. At the bottom they come back in the order you made them, and ring again if the sound is on.

How it is made

  • The floor is Depthfield's three ridges, drawn in one shader.
  • The shaft is noise in log-polar space, so it has no bottom to run out of.
  • Focus follows the pointer. Zeros blur by distance from it, using the texture's mipmaps.
  • The vanishing point holds its size with a dolly zoom: the lens widens as fast as you fall.
  • The frames are pentagons. Every sixth carries its pentagram, where diagonal to side is the golden ratio.
  • A lens pass bends the scene around the hole. The hole itself is drawn in exactly #07110f.
  • The sound is a Shepard–Risset glissando over a harmonic seventh. It falls without getting lower.

What is drawn

The eye is drawn, not sensing. It follows the pointer and nothing else. This page has no camera and no microphone, and sends nothing anywhere.
