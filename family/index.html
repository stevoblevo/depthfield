One drawing, sixteen looks

Storeybrook, Hypermetapunk and Depthfield are three design systems that keep one contract. Each takes the same canvas of flat fills and makes it in its own material, by a process and never by redrawing. Draw on the picture below and every plate follows.

Mood

A mood is what a look rewards in play: stillness by day, impact in print, a light source at night.

Storeybrook

Day · 1.5.0 · six looks

Paper first. Each look makes a picture the way a hand would: a wash, lamplight, brush ink, print, cut paper, cross-stitch. Each keeps one accent, in the emptiest corner.

    Hypermetapunk

    DUSK · 0.5.0 · THREE THEMES

    Printed in a hurry and lit by neon. A dot screen in Dusk, a photocopy with one red in Zine, a solid cut in High contrast. The accent is always the sound word.

      Depthfield

      NIGHT · 2.5.0 · SEVEN LOOKS

      The world behind the words. A scan for anything measured, with a readout as its accent; a stage for stories told at night, with a hanging star. Here the night faces scan and the day faces take the stage, footlit in their own accent.
