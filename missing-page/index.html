The census equates six lit windows with six occupied rooms. A note in its margin says the counter only reads the outer glass; covered light does not enter the count.

The section drawing shows three layers: Glass, Passage and Wall. Select the Wall, then the third position, “channel”. Room 07 appears behind it, borrowing passage light through a narrow channel.

The woven ledger records seven blankets and seven bowls issued to 07, no lamp oil, and no return before dawn. The room exists; the keepers deliberately omitted it. The incorrect census claim is “The seventh room does not exist.”

Extinguishing the passage leaves 07 dark. Lighting the outer window gives the counter a seventh light. Covering the outer pane while keeping the passage lit preserves both the refuge and the count of six.

Keep that arrangement. The missing page opens into a refuge. The keeper filed a wall where a room should be. At dawn, the census calls every counted light back to work.
