# Writing

The voice rules on the first page hold everywhere. These are the patterns, with the system's own lines as examples.

## Titles and eyebrows

- Chapter titles are two to four words with one italic word in the accent: "Cool *air*", "Light bends *green*", "The sky *turns*", "Breathe *out*".
- The opening title can be one word with a full stop: "Descend."
- Eyebrows number the chapter and name it: "Chapter 03 — Frost line". The first may name the place instead: "Depthfield · the glasshouse".

## Ledes and hints

- A lede says what to do and what happens, in that order: "Move and the petals part; press and the air ripples."
- One lede per beat, two sentences at most.
- A hint is one line in `cue` that invites and counts: "Three spores are hiding in the haze. 0 found." It changes when the count does, and closes the loop: "All three found. The scene is yours."
- "Scroll to change the weather." is a complete hint.

## Captions and finds

- A SceneCaption is a verb, a noun and two sentences: "Hold to open" / "Mint spore" / "A hotspot drawn into the art. Hover makes it bloom; press fires a ripple and counts it."
- A FoundCard counts, names, defines and tells one true thing, dry: "Discovered · 16/111" / "the bird" / "it sings over consonance" / "It only sings once the chord settles. It has standards."

## Actions and links

- Verbs, as the voice says: "Begin", "Enter", "Keep scrolling", "Skip intro", "Back to the top".
- Say where a link goes when it leaves: "Open the archive ↗"; doors say what kind of world and where it opens ("A narrative world. Opens in a new tab.").
- Private things say so: "Only you".

## When something is missing

Say what happened and where it works, without apology: "no motion sensors reached this page. the standalone page has them." "four voices is the limit." A refusal is information, not an error.

## Numbers and names

- Readouts pad and align: "042%", "02 / 05", "S07 / 14". Dates are ISO. Units go with the value.
- Names keep their own casing, even at the start of a sentence: a name set in capitals stays in capitals, a lowercase one stays lowercase.

## Words to leave out

In a page's own copy: "Experience", "journey", "immersive", "seamless", "delve", exclamation marks, emoji, "please", "simply", "just" as a softener, and any sentence that tells the reader how they will feel.
