# Writing

Plain-spoken, a little loud, never corporate. Hypermetapunk's words come from people who make odd things and want to be found.

- **Name things.** "Post a gig", "The atelier", "Twelve odd jobs tonight". Not "Get started", "Explore", "Solutions".
- **No slogans.** "Strange work finds its people" is a promise with a subject and a verb; "Work, reimagined" is a slogan.
- **Stickers shout one thing.** Two or three words: "no suits", "v2 louder", "open now".
- **Stamps state a status.** "approved", "sold out", "not the live site".
- **Scrawl is a person.** An aside in the first person, signed when it is someone in particular. Never the brand talking to itself.
- **Sentence case** everywhere except display lines, stickers, stamps and labels, which the styles set in capitals for you. Write them in sentence case in the markup.
