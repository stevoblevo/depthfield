# Patterns

## The scroll story

1. **Entry**: the opening beat with `df-h-xl`, a lede and one primary action (or a hint to scroll). A Loader only if the world needs real time.
2. **Chapters**: four to seven beats, each a ChapterMarker and a lede, alternating bottom and top anchors, one right-aligned at most every three.
3. **The turn**: one beat where the world changes the most (the weather turns, the floor drops, the focus pulls).
4. **The ending**: a quiet beat with a way out: Back to the top, or doors to other worlds.
5. **Footer** (optional): the closing statement and jump links.

The HUD runs the whole way: ScrollProgress at the start of the row (hidden on the entry beat), a HudChip or two at the end.

## Discovery: find N

A hint names the count; hotspots hide in the art; each press finds one (ripple, haptic, caption says "Found"); a HudChip counts ("spores **1** / 3"); a FoundCard confirms anything worth a name; the hint closes the loop when the count is full. Three to five things per scene; never a timer.

## A weather story

Four chapters, four weathers: peach fall (opening), red rain (the turn), mint clearing (the resolution; the eye opens), night garden (the ending; the doors appear). The accent follows; the HudChip names the weather; the eyebrow says "Then red rain" so the change is in words too.

## The guided reel

A tour of everything, for first visits and demos. Ten to fifteen shots of six to thirteen seconds; each has a name, a one-line tuning or caption, and sets one thing (a look, a view, a mode). Start it from a Button or the entry screen ("or watch the reel first"); any input hands it back with everything as it was; finishing it counts as a find. Hold toasts on phones until the end.

## Versions and the archive

A Ribbon across the top threads the versions (one knot each, the current one lit); a Tool opens the Drawer with every version, oldest first; a section of VersionCards closes the page. The current version is "here", never a link to itself.

## The reality lens

For pages that make claims about real systems. Every claim carries a Chip (observed, partial, in progress, proposed, missing, rejected, candidate); the lens Tool turns the page into a ledger (misregistered headings, flat art, scaled chips) and the LensBanner says so. Escape turns it off.

## Portals

Doors at the end of a story, drawn in the world (or as `df-door` links), at most three, each with a kind and a destination in its caption. They appear only in the last chapter.

## First-run hints

One at a time, two seconds after the world is ready, five seconds on screen, never again once acted on: the tilt hint ("tilt the phone: the voices roll like marbles…"), a controller's map, the hint for the first hotspot.

## Settings

A sheet or Drawer of Toggles and Sliders grouped under eyebrows (Motion, Sound, Looks), each with a one-line note under the group in `ink-muted` that says what is live right now ("sensors live, about 60 readings a second. tilt 12° right").

## Faces and skins

The face Tool (moon or sun) sits with the page's other tools; a skin picker belongs in settings, not the chrome. The high-contrast theme is offered next to the face tool or in settings, by name.
