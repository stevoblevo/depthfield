# Wave

The hyperreal half: surfaces that look rendered too well, at the hour the sun goes down.

## The horizon

The one scene. A band runs from the abyss at the top (`wave-abyss`) through indigo, violet and magenta to tangerine and an acid line at the horizon (`wave-sun`). The sun sits on that line, banded by bars that thin as they fall. Under it a grid floor in cyan and magenta runs toward you, one square every 3.2 seconds. Use it to open a page; close a page with doors on the void, not with a second horizon.

## Chrome

Display type cast in metal: the sky at the top of each letter, the horizon through its middle, the dusk at its foot (`chrome-1` to `chrome-5`). The light passes over it slowly. One or two lines a page; never small, never for body text.

## Glass

A pane lit from behind: `surface-raised` at 86%, blurred and saturated, with a neon edge in the accent. Glass holds what glows: a status, a door, a price. Paper holds what people read.

## Neon

`wave-mint` and `wave-cyan` are the signs and doors. Neon is a light source: it glows on the void (`shadow-glow`) and goes flat on Zine and High contrast.
