# Companions and sensing

A page can keep company: a guardian that follows and reviews, a state it infers instead of asking for, and a guide that counts what is left to find. Shipped first in Saelion Field (the strawberry) and Saelion Loom (the guardian on duty, the inferred-state chip, found 0 / 8), October 2026.

## The guardian

- One per page. It follows the hand at `depth-follow`, a little up and to the right, and looks at the pointer with one eye; it winks every four to nine seconds.
- Its voice is a reviewer's: short, dry, specific, never praise. Write its lines the way FoundCards are written. "Guardian on duty. Nothing gets adopted because I liked it." "Thrown, it finds a world." "Found the floor. Standards remain high."
- Press: it says its next line. Throw: it slides to a stop and lands on something (`onLand`), which may open it. Anything a throw opens has a link twin.
- It speaks rarely: on arrival, when pressed, when it lands, and at most once a minute about anything else (a state change, a find).
- The art keeps its own light: the strawberry and its bubble look the same in every theme. A page may draw its own guardian, keeping `data-df-pupil` and `data-df-lid` so it can look and wink.

## Inferred state

Depthfield infers rather than asks: people often do not know their own state, and a form that asks is a gate.

| State | When | A page might |
|---|---|---|
| arriving | before the first movement | show the opening at rest |
| calm | slow, steady movement | widen the stretch line; let the world breathe |
| curious | quicker, exploring | show the next hint |
| caffeinated | fast, or turning sharply and often | tense the stretch line; slow nothing down, say nothing |
| still | fourteen seconds without movement | dim the world a little; the guardian may say one line |

- Always label it **inferred**, in the chip and anywhere it is used. It is a guess from a pointer, not a reading of a person.
- Adapt quietly; never score, rank, gate or store it. Nothing leaves the page.
- A rehearsal (scripted signals chosen from a menu) is labelled scripted, not inferred. Keep the two apart.

## The guide

- A count ("found **2** / 8") and one hint: always the next step not yet found, in order. Steps run from obvious to hidden; the last may be a Secret.
- Each step is something done, not seen: pull, pluck, throw, flip the view, change the weather, hold still, find the egg, type the word.
- When the count is full, the hint closes the loop and the guardian may say one line.
- Never a timer, never a step a reader needs to use the page.
