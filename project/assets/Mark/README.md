# Mark

The Hypermetapunk mark: a banded dusk sun slapped onto a sticker, turned 6° and printed with a hard shadow. `hypermetapunk-mark.svg` is the dusk sticker for dark grounds; `hypermetapunk-mark-zine.svg` is the acid sticker for paper. Keep at least a quarter of its width clear around it, and never set it smaller than 24 px.
