# Door

A lit arch that goes somewhere: a real link drawn as a doorway, from Depthfield's doors, in neon.

**Provide** `<a class="hmp-door" href="…"><span class="hmp-door__arch" style="--door-c:var(--wave-mint)"></span><span class="hmp-door__cap">The atelier</span></a>`. `--door-c` sets its light (any `wave-*` or `punk-*` token); `--door-w` its width.

- Doors end a page or a room: three at most in a row, each to a different place.
- The caption is the destination's name.

**Accessibility**: a real link with a visible name; the arch lifts and brightens on hover and focus, and the focus ring is the system's.
