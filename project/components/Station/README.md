# Station

A numbered step in a long explanation: the numeral, the step (eyebrow + display title + paragraphs) and an aside of owners and notes.

**Provide** `<article class="df-station">` → `<span class="df-station__num">` (roman or arabic, italic display, `accent`) → `<div>` with `h3.df-station__title` (a `small` eyebrow inside it) and `df-station__text` paragraphs → `<aside class="df-station__aside">` with Tags, Chips or a note.

- Three columns wide, two below `bp-expanded` (the aside drops under the text), one on phones with a smaller numeral.
- Number stations in reading order; one idea each; the aside says who holds it and how real it is.
