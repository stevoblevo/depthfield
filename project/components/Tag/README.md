# Tag

Categorical labels: owners, worlds, kinds. Five tones, never used to mean good or bad.

**Provide** `<span class="df-tag df-tag--<tone>">Label</span>`; tones `gold` (default), `rose`, `violet`, `sea`, `peach`, plus `accent`. A tag is its tone at 12% over the ground, a 35% edge, a dot and the label in the tone.

- Keep a tone to one meaning across a site (for example: gold worlds, rose games, violet tools, sea stories, peach notes).
- The dot repeats the color so the label is never color-only; the word carries the meaning.
- Day faces use the tones' dark shades; in the thread day face small tag text on `surface` falls just under 4.5:1, so keep tags on `void` or `surface-raised` there.
