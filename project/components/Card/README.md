# Card

A sheet torn from a zine and taped over the art, or, for the neon side, a pane of glass lit from behind.

**Provide** `<article class="hmp-card">` (torn paper: `punk-paper` with `punk-ink` text in every theme but High contrast) or `<article class="hmp-card hmp-card--glass">` (surface glass with a neon edge). Add Tape, a Stamp and a Sticker on its corners as the content asks.

- Paper cards hold things people read closely: a gig, a brief, a receipt. Glass cards hold things that glow: a status, a price, a door.
- The torn edge is at the bottom; leave room under the last line.

**Accessibility**: paper cards keep ink on paper (16:1); glass cards use the theme's ink on `surface-raised`. A card is a container, not a button: put the link on its title.
