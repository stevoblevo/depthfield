# Punk

The hand-made half: everything that was printed cheaply, cut out, stuck on and written over.

## The slaps

- **Stickers** carry one or two words in Archivo Black on acid, orange, magenta, mint or cyan stock, with a 3 px black outline and a hard 4 px shadow, turned near 2.5°. Text on a sticker is always photocopy black.
- **Stamps** state a status in spaced Space Mono capitals inside a double border, struck at about 6°.
- **Tape** holds a card or a picture by its corners, translucent, two pieces at most.
- **Scrawl** is a person in the margin, in Permanent Marker: "← this is a website. it finds people work. — D."

## Placement

Slaps go on the frame: the top bar, a card's corners, beside a heading, in the margin. Never over running text; `Hypermetapunk.taste()` measures it. A frame with `data-hmp-slap` gives its stickers seeded tilts, so a page looks the same on every visit.

## Print faults, on purpose

- **Misregistration**: cyan and magenta plates slipped 2.5 px on display type and link labels, further when pointed at.
- **Grain**: photocopy noise at 0.16 over the horizon and cards (0.22 on Zine).
- **Halftone**: a dot screen for flat fills.
- **The torn edge**: paper cards end in a torn line at the bottom.

These are seasoning. A page where everything is misregistered, grained and taped is a costume.
